# सहायक Sahayak Nepal

**जहाँ सहयोग, त्यहाँ सहायक** (Where there is support, there is Sahayak)

Sahayak Nepal is an on-demand services platform that connects people in Nepal with trusted local help, anytime and anywhere. Whether your vehicle breaks down on the road or you need help at home, one app puts the right person a call away.

## Problem
In Nepal, finding a reliable mechanic, plumber, electrician or emergency help usually depends on personal contacts. This is slow and stressful, especially in emergencies or when you are stuck on the road.

## Solution
Sahayak Nepal brings essential everyday services into one platform.

## Services
- 🚗 Roadside vehicle mechanic
- 🔧 Plumber
- ⚡ Electrician
- 💧 Water tank cleaning
- 🧹 Maid / home cleaner
- 🚑 Ambulance
